# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- **Being respectful** — Different viewpoints and experiences are valued.
- **Being collaborative** — Work together to improve the project.
- **Being empathetic** — Gracefully accept constructive criticism.
- **Focusing on what is best** for the community.
- **Showing gratitude** towards contributors.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards.

### Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported by contacting the community team at **conduct@satisium-ui.com**.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

### Consequences

Community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining
the consequences for any action they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct:

| Violation                                | Consequence                                      |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **Correction** — minor infraction        | Private, written warning                         |
| **Warning** — single incident or series  | Warning with consequences for continued behavior |
| **Temporary Ban** — serious violation    | Temporary ban from interaction                   |
| **Permanent Ban** — pattern of violation | Permanent ban from community                     |

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also when an
individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.
