# Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

## 1. What Are Cookies

Cookies and local browser storage are small text files or key-value entries placed on your device to help web applications operate efficiently, preserve user preferences, and collect anonymous telemetry.

## 2. Cookies & Storage We Use

### Strictly Necessary & Preference Storage

These items are essential for the site to function or remember your choices:

- `theme`: Stores your preferred color mode (light, dark, or system preference) using `next-themes`.
- `satisium_consent_v1`: Stores your analytics and cookie consent choices so we do not repeatedly prompt you on subsequent visits.

### Analytics & Performance (Consent-Gated)

These services help us understand how visitors interact with our product and documentation:

- **PostHog**: Anonymous session metrics, page views, and feature usage. Only activated after you give explicit consent via our cookie banner.
- **Zero Third-Party Advertising**: We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or third-party ad networks.

## 3. Managing Your Preferences

You can update your cookie preferences at any time by clicking the consent settings link in our site footer or clearing your browser's local storage and cookies. Most browsers also allow you to block cookies through their settings.

## 4. Contact & Inquiries

If you have any questions about our use of cookies or local storage, please contact us at `legal@your-domain.com`.
