# Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

## 1. Overview

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data we handle. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit or use our services.

## 2. Analytics & Tracking

We run product analytics via PostHog to understand how our software and documentation are used.

- **Consent-Gated**: Analytics are only recorded if you accept our cookie & analytics banner.
- **Zero PII**: We do not collect names, email addresses, IP addresses, or sensitive project files in telemetry.
- **Opt-Out**: You can decline or change your consent at any time via the consent banner.

## 3. Third-Party Services

- **PostHog**: Anonymous usage analytics and pageviews (if consented).
- **Vercel**: Hosting and edge infrastructure.

## 4. Contact & Rights

If you have questions about this policy or wish to exercise data rights, contact us at `legal@your-domain.com`.
