Unlike static duration-based cubic-beziers, springs simulate actual physical forces. A spring does not have an arbitrary duration; its motion duration is an emergent property derived from physical dynamics.

## Physical Parameters

| Parameter     | Symbol | Range       | Description                                                                                |
| :------------ | :----- | :---------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Mass**      | $m$    | `0.1 – 5.0` | The inertia of the moving object. Higher mass creates heavier, more sluggish motion.       |
| **Stiffness** | $k$    | `1 – 500`   | The tension of the spring. Higher stiffness causes faster, snappier oscillations.          |
| **Damping**   | $c$    | `1 – 100`   | The friction resisting the motion. High damping suppresses oscillation (critical damping). |
